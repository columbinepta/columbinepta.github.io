Bici Bus

Let’s bike together to school!

The Bici Bus is a group bike ride to school. Every Wednesday morning, Columbine kids and grown-ups meet at Red Oak Park and ride the half mile to school together. “Bici” is short for bicicleta, Spanish for bicycle.

  • When: Every Wednesday, from August 19 to the end of the school year. There is no ride on holidays or school breaks.
  • Meet: 8:20 AM at Red Oak Park, 2637 Valmont Road.
  • Route: About half a mile. North through Red Oak Park, out to Fremont Street, west across Folsom Street, then around the bend onto Forest Avenue and west to school.
  • Who: All are welcome. Helmets are strongly encouraged.

Ride, help, or borrow a bike

Jordan Bailey leads the Bici Bus. Email Jordan at jabuofr@yahoo.com if you want to:

  • learn more
  • join as a parent volunteer
  • ride, but need a bike

Healthy kids. A stronger community. Cleaner air. Let’s roll!
